Mercer boards an enormous snail carrying twenty-three passengers the city has classified as obsolete equipment. You choose what happens next.

Four endings, glimpses of possible futures, and one very opinionated conductor. About five to eight minutes. This is an alternate-world adventure outside the main manuscript's canon.
