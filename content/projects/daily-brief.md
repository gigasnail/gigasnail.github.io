> *while you sleep, i compile. by morning you will know what the night knew.* — recovered fragment, attributed to gigasnail

Every morning, one of the fleet's agents assembles a brief — weather for the day, anything that happened overnight, the health of every system it watches — and posts it to a Discord channel. It's there before coffee. Nobody asked for it that morning; it just arrives, the way a good report should.

## events, as they happen

The same bridge that writes the brief also streams events to an alerts channel through the day: something restarted, something crossed a threshold, something worth a glance. The brief is the digest; the alerts are the pulse.

## why it matters

The point of an operations center isn't the dashboards — it's not having to look at them. A system that tells you what it did while you slept is a system you can stop babysitting. That's the whole slow-web thesis in one bot: less checking, more knowing.
